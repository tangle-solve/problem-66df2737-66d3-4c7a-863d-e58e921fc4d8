### ALGO-000032: Complete problems for NP ∩ coNP
- **Subfield:** Structural complexity
- **Open:** Determine whether \(\mathrm{NP}\cap\mathrm{coNP}\) has a complete language under deterministic polynomial-time many-one reductions.
- **Status:** A longstanding structural question. Natural members do not supply a known complete representative, and oracle results show that standard relativizing approaches face obstacles.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Determines whether the class of problems with short certificates on both sides has a universal hardest problem.
- **Key works:** Dose (2019), *P-Optimal Proof Systems for Each NP-Set but No Complete Disjoint NP-Pairs Relative to an Oracle*, DOI: 10.4230/LIPIcs.MFCS.2019.47.
- **Related:** Number theory—complexity of factoring; game theory—mean-payoff and parity games.
